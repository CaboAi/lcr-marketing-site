Questions

Is it free for renters? add

Joining the waitlist is free and nobody is charged for signing up. We'll publish any pricing before launch.

Which areas will it cover? add

Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, the Corridor and El Tezal at launch.

Can I see the place before I sign? add

Yes. Message the owner or agent in the app to set up a viewing before you sign anything.

What lease will I sign? add

A standard lease drafted for Baja California Sur, in Spanish and English. It covers rent, deposit, term, maintenance and early termination, and you can download it as a PDF.

How does the deposit work? add

You pay the deposit by card in the app when you sign. Monthly rent is arranged directly with your landlord.
