About

Why we're building Los Cabos Rental

Finding a long-term place in Los Cabos still runs through Facebook groups and WhatsApp. It can be simpler.

The problem

Most long-term rentals here are posted in Facebook groups. The same unit gets reposted again and again, often without a lease term, and sometimes with prices in dollars and pesos that don't match.

We're building one app where renters, landlords and agents can find each other, talk, and sign a lease, in English or Spanish.

From our pilot, Oct 2026

120 → 75

In one Los Cabos rental group, 120 rental posts came down to 75 distinct listings once repeats were merged.

The founder

Mario Polanco

Founder

Mario grew up in Los Angeles and lives in Los Cabos. He started building the app after seeing how much of the local long-term rental market runs through Facebook group posts.

Where things stand

The first version of the app is built but not public yet. It will open in Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, the Corridor and El Tezal, and the waitlist hears first.
