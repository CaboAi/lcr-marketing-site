Why we're building this

We read the rental groups so you don't have to.

Most long-term rentals in Los Cabos are posted in Facebook groups. In October 2026 we read through two of them, one for about a week and the other for about a day. Here's what we found.

199
posts read across the two groups (148 in the larger one over about a week, 51 in the other over about a day).
120 → 75
In the larger group, 120 rental posts came down to 75 distinct listings once repeats were merged.
4×
One 4-bedroom was posted four times in two days.
38 of 75
of those 75 listings didn't state a lease term. Only 33 clearly said long-term.
5
posters accounted for 56 of the 120 rental posts.
USD ≠ MXN
Some posts gave the rent in USD and MXN with figures that didn't match.

Pilot data from a one-week sample of two Los Cabos rental Facebook groups, October 2–10, 2026. One pull, read by hand.

The rule we're building toward: one address, one listing.

We're designing the app so each address is listed once, with the rent in pesos and the details in one place. When you're interested, you message the owner or agent directly in the app instead of chasing the same unit through five reposts.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free? add

Joining the waitlist is free and nobody is charged for signing up. Pricing for the app hasn't been decided yet.

When does it launch? add

Soon. We don't have a public date yet. The waitlist hears first, by email.

Which areas will it cover? add

Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, the Corridor and El Tezal at launch.

What happens after I sign up? add

Nothing until launch. Then you get one email with a link to create your account. We don't share your email with anyone.

Is it only for luxury rentals? add

No. It's for everyday long-term rentals: studios, apartments and houses, at local prices.

Is it for vacation rentals? add

No. Long-term rentals only, for people moving to Los Cabos to live.

Is the app in Spanish? add

Yes. The app and the lease are in both English and Spanish.
