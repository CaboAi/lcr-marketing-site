What does it cost? add

Joining the waitlist is free and nobody is charged for signing up. We'll announce pricing before launch.

How do I get paid? add

Directly from your tenant. Rent and the deposit are arranged between you and your tenant. The app doesn't process or hold payments.

Which areas will it cover? add

Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, the Corridor and El Tezal at launch.
